# Autonomy Policy

Date: 2026-10-05

This workflow is human-in-the-loop. Automation may gather evidence, run
deterministic checks, generate proposals, and execute bounded experiments, but
it cannot approve scientific truth or publish on the author's behalf.

## Automatic

- Recompute deterministic counts, hashes, citations, and reproducibility evidence.
- Run read-only lint, structure, provenance, and readiness checks.
- Record failed runs, dead ends, pivots, costs, and risks.
- Generate candidate ideas, experiment plans, review prompts, and rebuttal drafts.

## Human Approval Required

- Advancing a stage or accepting a gate after a failed check.
- Freezing experiment design or changing a primary endpoint.
- Accepting proof-audit, citation-support, figure-support, human-coding, and governance ledgers.
- Accepting same-family review override.
- Publishing a release, changing repository visibility, or pushing paper content.
- Declaring a research claim supported, provisional, or dropped.

## External Review

High-assurance work requires an external human challenge review and independent
proof review. Model review can drive the process and surface evidence, but it
cannot acquit a claim.
